Mpumalanga Basic Traffic Officers Diploma Learnership 2026/27

learnershipMpumalanga

A 12-month Basic Traffic Officers Diploma learnership at Mpumalanga Traffic Training College in Mkhuhlu (Calcutta), Bushbuckridge Local Municipality. The official advert lists a stipend of R5 000 per month and a closing date of 30 October 2026. The advert does not state an employment-status restriction; applicants should confirm any eligibility uncertainty with the department.

Opportunity details

Sector / SETA: Mpumalanga Department of Community Safety, Security and Liaison

Minimum qualification: Matric

Stipend / funding: R5 000/month

Closing date: 30 October 2026

Posted: 13 October 2026

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Eligibility

  • Grade 12 certificate
  • South African citizen residing in Mpumalanga
  • At least a Code B driving licence
  • No criminal record
  • Fit and proper to participate in strenuous training; shortlisted candidates undergo a medical assessment
  • Must be prepared for driving and competency tests

Documents to prepare

  • Completed new Z83 application form
  • Detailed CV including at least three referees and their contact details
  • Copies of qualifications and other supporting documents if shortlisted, as directed by the advert
