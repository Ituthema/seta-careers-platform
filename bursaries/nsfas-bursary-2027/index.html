NSFAS Bursary 2027 — University & TVET Funding

bursaryNationwide

NSFAS applications for the 2027 academic year are open from 18 September to 31 October 2026. Eligible students at public universities and TVET colleges can apply for funding through the official myNSFAS portal. Applications are free; applicants should confirm their personal eligibility and required documents directly with NSFAS.

Opportunity details

Sector / SETA: Government – DHET

Minimum qualification: Matric

Stipend / funding: Full bursary – varies by institution

Closing date: 31 October 2026

Posted: 18 September 2026

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Eligibility

  • South African citizen
  • Household income below R350 000/year
  • Registered or applying at a public university or TVET college
  • SASSA grant recipients qualify automatically

Documents to prepare

  • South African ID
  • Proof of income (payslips or affidavit)
  • Matric results or academic record
  • Proof of registration at institution
