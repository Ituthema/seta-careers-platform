Bursaries in South Africa

Current listings are shown below. Confirm eligibility, deadlines, and application instructions with the official provider before applying.

NSFAS Bursary 2027 — University & TVET Funding

NSFAS applications for the 2027 academic year are open from 18 September to 31 October 2026. Eligible students at public universities and TVET colleges can apply for funding through the official myNSFAS portal. Applications are free; applicants should confirm their personal eligibility and required documents directly with NSFAS.

Nationwide · Matric · Closing 31 October 2026

Funza Lushaka Bursary 2027 — First-Time Applicants

The Department of Basic Education says Funza Lushaka Bursary applications for 2027 opened on 1 October 2026. This listing is for first-time applicants, whose closing date is 31 January 2027; returning applicants have an earlier 30 November 2026 deadline. The bursary supports teacher education and covers specified study and living costs. Apply through the official eServices portal and confirm the current requirements there.

Nationwide · Matric with a bachelor's pass or university-entry requirements · Closing 31 January 2027
