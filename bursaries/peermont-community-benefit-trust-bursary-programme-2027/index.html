Peermont Community Benefit Trust Bursary Programme 2027

Unverified: this listing has not yet been checked against the official advert. Confirm the details with the provider before applying.

bursaryGauteng

The Peermont Community Benefit Trust offers bursaries to young people who permanently live in the Ekurhuleni Metropolitan area and plan to study full-time at undergraduate level. The trust says the bursary covers tuition and registration fees, accommodation, and prescribed books or study materials. The trust no longer funds medicine. This listing relies on NWU's external bursaries page; the short application link is the one NWU publishes. Confirm all details with the trust before applying.
