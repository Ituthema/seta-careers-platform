Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

In short: OpportunitiesZA has no accounts, no sign-up forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising. We do not ask for your ID, CV, certificates or any other document. Saved opportunities stay in your own browser.

1. Who we are

OpportunitiesZA is an independent information website run by an individual in South Africa. We publish learnerships, bursaries, internships and apprenticeships and link to the official source of each. We are not an employer, recruiter or government department. You can contact us at contact@setacareersportal.abrdns.com.

2. What the website collects

Nothing that identifies you. The site has no registration, no newsletter or other sign-up form, and no comment or upload feature. We do not use analytics or advertising tools, and the site does not set cookies.

3. What stays on your device

If you save an opportunity, the site keeps its identifier in your browser’s local storage so the list is still there next time. It is never sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing the site’s data in your browser.

4. Our hosting and other services

  • GitHub Pages. The site is hosted by GitHub, Inc. Like any web host, GitHub receives technical details of each request (such as your IP address and browser type) and handles them under its own privacy statement. We do not receive visitor-level logs and do not use them to identify anyone.
  • Google Fonts. The interactive version of the site loads its fonts from Google (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), so your browser sends Google a request that includes your IP address. The simple static pages (listing pages, guides, this page) do not load anything from other sites.

GitHub and Google are based in the United States, so this technical data may be processed outside South Africa.

5. If you email us

We use your email address and message only to reply, to look into a scam report or to consider a listing you suggest. We do not share it, and we delete it when it is no longer needed. Please do not send ID numbers, certificates or other sensitive documents by email: we do not need them.

6. Links to other websites

Each listing links to the official page where you apply. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control. Before you enter personal details anywhere, check that the address belongs to the organisation named in the advert. No genuine learnership, bursary or internship asks you to pay to apply.

7. What we never do

  • Sell, rent or trade information about visitors.
  • Ask you for documents or payment.
  • Profile you for advertising.

8. Your rights (POPIA)

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to our processing it. Apart from emails you send us, we hold none. Write to contact@setacareersportal.abrdns.com. If you are unhappy with our response you may complain to the Information Regulator.

9. Children

The site is meant for school leavers and other job and study seekers. Because we collect no personal information through it, we hold none about children.

10. Changes

If we ever add analytics, a newsletter or anything else that collects information, we will update this page first and change the date at the top.
