LH Marthinusen Apprenticeship Applications — Phalaborwa Branch

apprenticeshipLimpopo

LH Marthinusen is accepting apprenticeship applications for its Phalaborwa branch. The official form lists Armature Winder, Electrician, Welder, and Fitter and Turner trades. Minimum requirement: N2 / NCV Level 4 with four completed subjects relevant to the trade. A CV in Word or PDF format and supporting documents are required. Applications close 15 November 2026. The employer explicitly asks candidates not to submit multiple applications across branches or trades.
