1. What this website is

OpportunitiesZA is an independent information service. We are not an employer, recruiter, training provider, SETA, bursary provider or government department, and we are not affiliated with the organisations whose opportunities we list. We do not take applications and cannot influence who is selected.

2. Accuracy and verification

  • Listings summarise information from the official source we link to. Closing dates, requirements and availability can change without notice, and we may be slow to catch a change.
  • A listing marked Unverified has not been fully confirmed against an official page. Treat it as a lead and check the source yourself.
  • Where an advert does not state something (such as the stipend or required documents) we say it is not stated rather than guess.
  • The official advert always overrides what we publish.

3. No fees, no guarantees

We never charge to view listings and we do not charge applicants. We do not promise that you will qualify for, or be offered, any opportunity. Our checkers and calculators give a rough indication only and are not a decision by any provider.

4. Scams

Genuine learnerships, bursaries and internships do not ask applicants to pay. Be careful with anyone who asks for money, a fee for a “placement” or documents sent to a personal email or WhatsApp number. If you see anything claiming to be us that does this, report it to contact@setacareersportal.abrdns.com.
