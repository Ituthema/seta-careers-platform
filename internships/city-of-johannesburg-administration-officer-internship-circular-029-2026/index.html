City of Johannesburg Administration Officer Internship — Circular 029/2026

internshipGauteng

An internship of up to 18 months supporting the Office of the Executive Director in the Environmental and Infrastructure Services Department. Published 22 October 2026; the circular lists a basic salary of R10,109.86 per month with no benefits. Only qualifying residents of the City of Johannesburg will be considered. Applications close 4 November 2026.

Opportunity details

Sector / SETA: City of Johannesburg — Environmental and Infrastructure Services Department

Minimum qualification: Grade 12 plus a 3-year qualification (360 credits, NQF Level 6 or equivalent)

Stipend / funding: R10,109.86/month (basic salary, no benefits)

Closing date: 4 November 2026

Posted: 22 October 2026

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Eligibility

  • Grade 12 (NQF Level 4) plus a three-year qualification with 360 credits in Law, Administration, Public Governance, Business Management or Business Administration at NQF Level 6 or equivalent
  • Must be a resident of the City of Johannesburg
  • Must be a qualifying student in the Johannesburg community
  • Must not have participated in an internship programme before, except where the previous programme was not relevant to the candidate’s course of study

Documents to prepare

  • Certified qualification(s)
  • South African identity document
  • CV
  • Completed online application
